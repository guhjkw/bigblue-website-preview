Discover Vanuatu’s Underwater World

With Big Blue Dive Vanuatu

Centrally located on the seawall in Port Vila, Vanuatu’s capital city, Big Blue is Vanuatu’s only PADI 5 Star Instructor Development Dive Resort, offering professional, friendly service to every stripe of underwater enthusiast. We take divers of all levels out to some of Vanuatu’s most amazing reefs and wrecks.

Don’t dive yet, but looking to get your toes wet? We can teach you! And if you want to go all the way, and become an instructor, we are one of only a handful of centres in the South Pacific which run Instructor Development Courses.

We are a Project Aware 100% Aware Partner dive centre which means a contribution is made to ocean conservation when a student is certified.

We’re here to show you everything Vanuatu’s waters have to offer, from a quick dip on your cruise ship stop to customised packages. For groups of six or more we offer a dedicated boat and divemaster or instructor. For more details, get in touch.
