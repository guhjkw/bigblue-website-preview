Why Port Vila

A harbour full of reefs, wrecks and surprises

Port Vila sits on a sheltered harbour inside a wide bay. For divers that means more than 20 sites around Port Vila, from shallow coral gardens to wrecks for experienced divers.

We pick each day's sites that morning, looking at the wind, weather and tides, the experience of everyone on board, and where you have already dived with us. Every now and then a dugong drops in to say hello.

A few of our favourite sites

  • Konanda Wreck · 26 m

    A 45 m former island trader sitting upright on sand. You can explore the holds, cabin and bridge. A great first wreck.

  • Star of Russia Wreck · 34 m

    A 90 m square-rigged sailing ship from Harland and Wolff, the yard behind the Titanic. Schools of fish live inside. For experienced divers.

  • Semele Federesen Wreck · 30–55 m

    An inter-island cargo ship lying on sand, with the top of the wreck at 30 m. Visibility can reach 40 to 50 m. For experienced divers.

  • The Cathedral Cavern · 22 m

    A wide limestone cavern lit by shafts of sunlight through its open roof, then a wall swim back to the boat. Calm days only.

  • Twin Bommies Reef · 12–30 m

    Two coral bommies on a plateau that draw big crowds of fish. Look for nudibranchs and leaf scorpionfish, and turtles at night.

  • Pink Panther Wall · from 8 m

    Giant pink anemones full of clownfish on top of a wall that drops past 90 m. Keep an eye on the blue.

Also on the list: Kate's Corner, West Side Story, Yankee Juliette and more.

Cruise-ship divers

Two dives on your day in port

Most ships dock at 8am, a little late for our regular morning boat. So on ship days we set aside a boat just for passengers, with time for two dives and the trip back to your ship.

  1. 8:00

    Your ship arrives

    Everyone comes ashore at the wharf, even if your ship anchors in the harbour.

  2. 8:30–10:30

    Meet us on the seafront

    Make your own way to our shop. A water taxi across the harbour is often the easiest way. We can't collect from the wharf, as pick-ups there are reserved for local transport operators.

  3. 2 dives

    Your own boat and guide

    A PADI Divemaster or Instructor shows you the best sites. Fresh fruit and locally made cookies between dives.

  4. 12:30–2:30

    Back on the seafront

    Back ashore with time to get to your ship.

Our story

Local divers, local knowledge

Mike and Maggie Crawford started Big Blue in 2005. Mike spent years training Ni-Vanuatu divers all the way to divemaster and instructor, and that is still at the heart of the team today. Many of our crew grew up in these waters and love showing them off.
